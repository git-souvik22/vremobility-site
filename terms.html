Terms & Conditions

These terms govern use of the VRE Mobility app and website operated by VRE GAME.

1. Our service

VRE Mobility is a technology platform connecting passengers with independent captains for bike, auto and cab rides. Captains are not employees of VRE GAME.

2. Accounts

You sign in with your mobile number and a one-time password. You must be 18 or older and are responsible for activity on your account.

3. Rides

Each ride has a 4-digit passcode which you share with your captain only once seated in the correct vehicle.

4. Fares

Fares are shown before booking and paid directly to the captain by UPI or cash. VRE GAME does not collect or hold ride fares.

5. Captain membership

Captains must pass KYC review and hold an active membership to accept rides. Membership is a prepaid plan for a fixed number of days and is processed by Razorpay.

6. Conduct

Fraud, abuse or false KYC details lead to suspension.
