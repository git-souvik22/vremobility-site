Delete your VRE Mobility account

You can permanently delete your VRE Mobility account (operated by VRE GAME) at any time.

Delete from the app

  1. Open the VRE Mobility app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Delete by email

If you can't access the app, email hello@vremobility.com from any address with the subject “Account deletion request” and your registered mobile number. We process requests within 7 days.

What is deleted

Your name, mobile number, captain KYC details (licence, vehicle, UPI ID) and membership are deleted. Anonymised ride and payment records (with no link to your identity) are kept for up to 180 days for safety, fraud-prevention and legal/tax reasons, then removed.

Note: active rides must be completed or cancelled before deletion. Membership fees already paid are not refunded on deletion.
