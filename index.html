VRE Mobility is a ride-booking app by VRE GAME that connects passengers with verified, independent captains for bike, auto and cab rides across India.

🛵 Book a ride

Choose pickup and drop on the map, see the fare upfront and get matched with a nearby captain.

📍 Live tracking

Track your captain in real time, chat or call in-app, and start the ride with a secure passcode.

💸 Pay the captain directly

Pay the ride fare straight to your captain by UPI or cash — no hidden charges.

🧑‍✈️ Drive with us

Captains complete KYC verification and activate a membership plan to accept rides and keep 100% of their fares.

Our services & pricing

Passengers: booking is free; ride fares are calculated by distance and vehicle type, shown before booking and paid directly to the captain.

Captains: a paid membership plan (shown in the app, in Indian Rupees) is required to go online and accept rides. Membership is paid securely online via Razorpay.

Get the app

VRE Mobility is available as an Android app. For support, see our Contact page.
